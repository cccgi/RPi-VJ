🚀 Transfer Show & Media to Raspberry Pi 5

Choose your preferred transfer method to copy all video loops, thumbnails, shaders, and show.toml to the Pi 5:

Option 1: 1-Click USB Thumb Drive (Easiest)

Copy the entire show bundle to a USB thumb drive. When plugged into the Pi 5, it auto-mounts and launches automatically.

cargo run -p vj-ingest -- export-usb ./my_show E:\

Option 2: Direct Network Sync (Wi-Fi / Ethernet)

Sync all files directly to your Pi 5 over your local network in seconds without touching the USB drive:

cargo run -p vj-ingest -- sync ./my_show pi@192.168.1.150
