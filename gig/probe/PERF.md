## Reading the failures
Failing rows land on exact vsync subdivisions (60/2, 60/3, 60/5, 60/6, 60/8, 60/10).
That quantization is the signature of genuine missed frames — the measurement is sound.

## Platform facts (from probe.html)
- `EXT_color_buffer_float`: **available**
- `OES_texture_float_linear`: **NOT available** — float FBOs cannot be linearly
  filtered. **G-12's bloom downsample chain must use RGBA8**, or nearest on float.
- `MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE`: 4096
- `MAX_FRAGMENT_UNIFORM_VECTORS`: 1024 · `MAX_VARYING_VECTORS`: 16

## Shader convention (IMPORTANT — not stock ISF)
These shaders declare their own uniforms and use `gl_FragColor`:
`u_time`, `u_resolution`, `u_bass`, `u_mid`, `u_treble`, `u_onset`, `u_volume`, `u_beat_phase`
The `/*{...}*/` ISF header's `INPUTS` entries (e.g. `flow_speed`) are NOT declared in
the body and must be generated by the loader. There is no `TIME`, `RENDERSIZE`, or
`isf_FragNormCoord` usage. `isf.js` (G-04) must target this convention.

`isf_FragNormCoord`, if provided, MUST be a `#define` — a global initializer fails
with `'=' : global variable initializers must be constant expression`.
